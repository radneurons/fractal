# Phase 6 — Advanced

**Built**
- **Webcam mocap** (Live tab):
  - MediaPipe Pose runs in the browser and records world landmarks;
  - `retargetFrame` solves per-joint local rotations by aligning each bone's rest direction with the observed one, hierarchically, and recovers facing as root yaw;
  - cleanup with One-Euro-style smoothing and foot locking;
  - the result becomes a new clip on the selected character and is assigned to the key at the playhead.
- **Phone virtual camera**:
  - pairing by QR code (the LAN address comes from the bridge);
  - the phone streams DeviceOrientation over a WebRTC data channel (unordered, no retransmits), signalled through the bridge's `/phone` relay, which doubles as the fallback transport;
  - Calibrate matches the phone to the selected camera; aiming previews live; Record samples 12 keys per second of scene time while the scene plays, as one undoable entry.
- **Audio**: import a file; waveform peaks draw in the timeline; onset detection plus autocorrelation tempo produce beat markers, and keys and shot edges snap to them.
- **Yjs collaboration**:
  - every local op (including undo/redo as inverse ops) is appended to a shared Y.Array;
  - each peer's project is the snapshot plus the log in Yjs order, so peers converge; an op that a concurrent edit invalidated is skipped identically everywhere;
  - presence names and 3D cursors are relayed by the bridge (`/collab`), which also keeps the doc for late joiners.
- Shipped in earlier phases and listed here because the brief puts them in Phase 6:
  - terrain sculpt (raise/lower/smooth/flatten) and the scatter brush (Scene view modes);
  - effects proxies (dust, smoke, sparks, rain, slash arcs) with their own mask pass;
  - region inpaint from a painted mask (Takes);
  - storyboard PDF with notes written by Claude (Takes → Assembly).

**Tested**
- `pnpm test`: mocap round trip (poses generated by the rig → landmarks → retarget → within 5 cm at hands and feet), facing recovery, clip build; beat detection on a 120 BPM click track; two peers editing concurrently, including a conflicting delete/rename, converge to identical projects; late joiner.
- `pnpm test:e2e` (starts a real bridge):
  - two browser contexts collaborate in one room, and an undo propagates;
  - a simulated phone pairs, streams over WebRTC, calibrates and records a pan into camera keys;
  - an audio file is imported and its beats detected at 120 BPM.

**Known gaps**
- Mocap needs network access on first use (the MediaPipe WASM and model come from public CDNs) and a webcam, so CI tests the solver, not the camera path. Hands and face are not captured; landmarks carry no bone twist.
- iOS only exposes motion sensors over HTTPS; serve the studio with a certificate (for example `vite --https`) for iPhones.
- Collaboration undo is per user and inverse-based, so undoing your edit after someone else changed the same thing can be skipped as a conflict (it is reported, not silently applied).
- Panels resize and the layout persists, but panels cannot be re-docked into other positions.
