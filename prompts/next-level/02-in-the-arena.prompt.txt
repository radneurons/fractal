The doer's clauses ("The credit belongs…who strives valiantly;") are on the arena floor:

- Each letter is a real recessed volume with walls, floor, and cast shadow. Build them by extruding glyph outlines downward into the floor mesh; triangulate the outlines with an ear-clipping or earcut routine embedded in the file.
- ARENA is the largest word in the film.

- Add a soft hemisphere fill.
- Add atmospheric dust: a height-limited fog raymarched in a screen pass, its density shaped by seeded 3D noise, glowing where it crosses the sun. It must be a function of t, never accumulated.

On "whose face is marred by dust and sweat and blood," three material passes act on the deep-cut floor lettering of FACE, which sits beside ARENA. Each completes before the next begins:
- DUST: grit accumulates on the letters' upper faces. Drive it with a noise-thresholded mask whose coverage rises with progress. The roughness rises too. Faint motes lift from the edges.
- SWEAT: a wet clearcoat sweeps across the letters, catching the low sun as a moving specular streak. Darken the albedo slightly where it is wet.
- BLOOD: dark red seeps into the recessed letter floors and pools along the inner walls, flowing to the lowest points first. The fill level is driven by the letter's depth and its distance to the walls. It reads as color filling engraving, not as gore.
All three remain visible to the end.
